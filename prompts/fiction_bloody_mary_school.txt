# BLOODY MARY: THE BATHROOM RUMOR

Write an ORIGINAL fictional horror story about an adult remembering a Bloody Mary rumor from an old school building.

The story is fictional. Do not portray the legend as a real supernatural event. Do not recreate the standard mirror ritual scene.

Instead, use the rumor as the beginning of a different mystery: an abandoned bathroom, a message scratched into a mirror, a missing object, a locked room, or another concrete clue.

Build toward a specific explanation or twist and end on a visual detail the viewer can picture.

Adults only. No harm to children, no gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, real people, real brands, or named towns.
