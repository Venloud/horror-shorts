# Selected Night Files Script: The Footprints Led In. Nobody Came Out.

**Production target:** 61 to 68 seconds, 1080 × 1920, factual historical mystery.
**Editorial choice:** User's script is stronger than Nina's earlier script for timed scenes, sound design and visual specificity. This version retains its structure while distinguishing reported claims from established facts. Estimated 141 spoken words, verify actual duration with TTS.

In March 1922, a Bavarian farmer reportedly found footprints leading from the woods to his isolated home. But no tracks led back out.

Then came more strange warnings. Footsteps supposedly echoed through the attic. A newspaper appeared that nobody recognized. And a house key was missing.

On March thirty-first, six people were murdered at Hinterkaifeck. Four bodies were found in the barn, and two inside the farmhouse, including the newly arrived maid.

But the strangest detail came afterward.

There were indications somebody had stayed on the property after the killings. The animals had apparently been tended, and smoke was reported from the chimney.

Was the murderer hiding nearby before the attack? Or were these clues misunderstood?

Investigators never established who killed the six residents. More than one hundred years later, the footsteps, the footprints, and the question of who was there remain part of the mystery.
