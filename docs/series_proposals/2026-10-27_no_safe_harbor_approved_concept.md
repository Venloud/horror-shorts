# No Safe Harbor, owner concept approval, 2026-10-27

Status: OWNER APPROVED CONCEPT AND THREE EPISODE OUTLINE ONLY. No script, character artwork, shot plan, media render, publishing sequence, or automatic release approval. Fiction, not a true historical event.

Owner's approved premise: Survivors on a boat at sea hear breaking news on the radio of a new virus and zombie outbreak ashore. SOS calls overwhelm the channels. Coastal land and ports are unsafe to return to. The story asks how to survive offshore when fuel, fresh water, food and safe landing points are uncertain.

Working series title: No Safe Harbor
Working series ID: no-safe-harbor-01 (proposed, not activated)
Format: one original three episode Night Files horror survival trilogy, ordinary standalones remain default.

Episode 1, Don't Return to Shore: an emergency broadcast first instructs return, then warns boats to remain offshore; panicked SOS traffic and a nearby infected vessel reveal the danger. Low fuel and approaching weather force a real decision.
Episode 2, The Ocean Isn't Safe Either: after two days, fresh water falls low and a drifting trawler presents a high-risk resource opportunity; a recording points toward an evacuation island.
Episode 3, The Last Safe Place: the island evacuation point is overrun. Survivors take a risky limited shore trip for essential supplies and retreat to sea with a short-term fishing, rainwater and rationing plan. The sea buys time, it is not a permanent miracle solution.

Creative continuity constraints: same primary boat and fuel/water inventory; consistent survivors, radio and SOS progression; no arbitrary cure, conveniently safe land, fake documented outbreak, or unsupported new equipment. Each installment has an earned local payoff; final resolves the immediate survival problem but does not pretend that long-term survival is guaranteed.
