# BLOODY MARY: AFTER THE STORY

Write an ORIGINAL fictional horror story inspired by what happens AFTER someone hears the Bloody Mary legend.

Do not show the standard ritual. The story should begin with an ordinary adult noticing one impossible or disturbing detail after hearing the legend. Escalate through concrete evidence and end with a reveal that recontextualizes the first detail.

The Bloody Mary reference should be inspiration, not proof that the supernatural exists. The ending should work as psychological horror even if the viewer interprets the event rationally.

Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people, real brands, or named towns.
