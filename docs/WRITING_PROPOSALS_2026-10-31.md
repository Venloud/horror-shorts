# Night Files writing proposals (owner + Claude, 2026-10-31) — NOT implemented yet, awaiting owner's go per item

Goal: scripts worth saving ("what value does this give me?"), not the same beats every time.

1. **Value first** — before writing, pick ONE takeaway the viewer keeps (the real mechanism, what to do if..., the
   real explanation behind the legend). A writing check flags a draft that never delivers it (one rewrite).
2. **Angle before script** — pitch 5 one-sentence angles ("why would someone care"), score curiosity + value,
   write only the best.
3. **No repeats** — each run reads the last 30 scripts on main and gives the writer a "don't say" list of its own
   overused openings / closers / question patterns. Max one question, and only if the next line answers it.
4. **Stronger writer for the script step only** (paid model, cents per video) — needs the owner's yes.
5. **3-part series** — trilogy gate exists (disabled); wait until 1-3 make single videos good.

## Formats to try (owner, Oct 31)

6. **"Your life as..." POV** — tell a real person's story as if the viewer IS them ("You're nine. Your father
   makes you rehearse with your brothers every night..."), instead of "you watch her do this". For true stories:
   only sourced events; never invented thoughts presented as fact (frame inner life as "imagine").
   Trial as a mode variant, not a full switch; compare analytics.
7. **"What would actually happen if..."** — a step-by-step scenario ("if you were being chased by X: first...,
   then..."), for legends / fiction where the source gives the rules. Fiction labelled as fiction.
8. **Second hook ("until...")** — one planned re-hook around 40-50% of the runtime, where attention dips: a turn
   word ("until", "then", "except") + a new stake, then the payoff. One hook up front that catches, one re-hook
   that keeps; no double hook at the start.
