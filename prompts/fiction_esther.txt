# QUEEN ESTHER: BIBLICAL / HISTORICAL MYSTERY

Write a tense, cinematic retelling centered on the biblical story of Esther.

This is a biblical/historical story, not an invented modern horror story. Keep the major events grounded in the Book of Esther. Do not invent supernatural events, secret conspiracies, or new historical facts and present them as scripture or history.

Focus on the mystery and danger surrounding Esther's hidden identity, the royal court, Haman's plot, Mordecai's warnings, Esther's decision to approach the king, and the reversal that follows.

The narration should feel suspenseful and atmospheric rather than like a sermon. You may use carefully framed dramatic reconstruction of scenes, but do not put invented dialogue in quotation marks as if it were scripture.

End on a concrete detail, reversal, or unanswered historical question that leaves the viewer thinking.

FACT RULES:
- Clearly distinguish the biblical account from dramatic interpretation.
- Do not claim details that the biblical text does not establish.
- Adults only.
- No gore, sexual content, self-harm, or harm to children.
- No modern brands or invented modern locations.
