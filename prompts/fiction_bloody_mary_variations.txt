# BLOODY MARY: THE VERSION YOU HAVEN'T HEARD

Write a cinematic Night Files story about ONE lesser-known variation of the Bloody Mary folklore.

Do not present several versions as a list. Pick one variation and tell it as a scene with a person hearing or learning the legend, a specific unsettling detail, escalation, and a final image.

The variation must be framed as folklore, not verified supernatural fact. Do not invent a regional origin and claim it is historically documented.

The goal is to make viewers who already know the usual mirror story feel like they learned something new.

Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people, real brands, or named towns.
