# THE PHOTOGRAPH WITH ONE EXTRA PERSON

Write an original mystery-horror story about an old family photograph containing one person nobody remembers.

The narrator investigates the photograph through concrete details: clothing, position, reflections, dates, albums, handwriting, or other physical evidence.

The mystery should escalate without becoming a generic ghost story. The final image or discovery should provide a chilling payoff.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
