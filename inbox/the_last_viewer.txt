SCRIPT
There's a horror movie you should never watch alone.
Not because of what happens in the movie...
But because of what happens after.

A man named Daniel found an old horror movie online.
No reviews.
No studio name.
Just one title: "THE LAST VIEWER."

He pressed play.
The movie showed a man sitting alone in a dark living room.
Daniel immediately noticed something strange.
The room looked exactly like his.
Same couch.
Same lamp.
Same television.

Daniel paused the movie.
The man on the screen paused too.
Daniel leaned closer.
The man leaned closer.
Then the man slowly looked directly at him.

He whispered, "Don't turn around."

Daniel laughed.
Then his TV went completely black.

Behind him...
Someone whispered his name.

Daniel froze.
His phone suddenly lit up.
One message.
"Why did you stop watching?"

Daniel looked back at the television.
The movie was playing again.
But this time...
the man on the couch was gone.

Because Daniel was sitting there instead.

And standing behind him...
was the same man from the movie.
