# THE STRANGER WHO KNEW ME

Write an original psychological horror story about an adult who repeatedly encounters the same stranger in different ordinary places.

The stranger gradually reveals knowledge of memories, routines, or private details the narrator never told anyone.

Keep the threat believable and grounded. Do not rely on supernatural powers unless the story earns them through concrete evidence.

Build toward a specific final discovery that changes the meaning of the earlier encounters.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
