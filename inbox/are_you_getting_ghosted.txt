My girlfriend stopped replying on Tuesday.

No calls. No texts. Nothing.

By Friday, I figured she'd ghosted me.

Then, at 2:13 AM, she sent a picture.

It was my bedroom.

Taken from the corner near my closet.

I called immediately.

No answer.

Another picture arrived.

This one showed me sleeping.

I checked the closet.

Empty.

Then my phone buzzed again.

A message said, "Stop looking for me."

I asked where she was.

Three dots appeared.

Then a final picture loaded.

It was taken from underneath my bed.

In the reflection of my bedroom mirror, I could see someone standing behind me.

My girlfriend.

Except she'd been reported missing three days earlier.

And whatever was wearing her face was smiling.
