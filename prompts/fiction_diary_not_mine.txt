# THE DIARY THAT WASN'T MINE

Write an original mystery-horror story about an adult who finds a diary written decades before their birth that accurately describes their current life.

Use specific entries and physical evidence to escalate the mystery. The diary should contain details that could not reasonably have been known when it was written.

Avoid a generic reincarnation or dream explanation. End with a new entry, physical discovery, or contradiction that makes the mystery worse.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
