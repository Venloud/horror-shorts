# THE HOUSE THAT ADDED A ROOM

Write an original psychological horror story about an adult who notices that a new door or room has appeared inside their home.

Start with a small physical inconsistency and escalate through concrete evidence. The narrator should investigate the impossible room while trying to understand whether it belongs to the house.

Avoid a generic dream or hallucination explanation. The ending should reveal one disturbing fact about the room that cannot be easily explained.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
