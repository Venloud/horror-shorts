# THE BOOK THAT KNEW MY NAME

Write an original psychological horror story about an adult who finds an old book that begins describing their life with impossible accuracy.

The book should first seem like an ordinary strange book. Then it describes the narrator's apartment, routine, or recent actions. It eventually contains the narrator's name and begins predicting what they are doing while they read.

Build tension through concrete discoveries: pages, handwriting, timestamps, objects, rooms, or changes in the text.

The final reveal should be unsettling and specific. Avoid a generic dream twist, hallucination explanation, or simple "they died" ending.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
