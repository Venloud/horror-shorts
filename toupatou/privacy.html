Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Prelaunch preview

This policy applies to the current Toupatou publishing demonstration. It will require an update before OAuth account connections, user registrations, video storage or production publishing are enabled.

Information entered in the preview

Captions and publishing selections in the current demonstration are processed within your browser to show the preview result. This demonstration does not transmit these inputs to TikTok or to a Toupatou publishing server.

Hosting data

Once a web host is configured, that host may process your IP address, browser information, and access logs to deliver the website and protect it from abuse. The hosting provider and retention policies will be disclosed before launch.

Account authorization and TikTok data

The current preview does not connect TikTok accounts, collect TikTok access tokens, or read TikTok profile information. Before OAuth is launched, Toupatou will explain what account data is collected, what permissions it requests, how long information is kept, how access is revoked, and how data can be deleted.

Uploaded content and sharing

The current preview does not upload, store or publish video files. The actual publishing service will require separate disclosures about media storage, schedules, authorized sharing with TikTok and retention before it is made available.

Cookies and analytics

The present demonstration does not implement advertising trackers or a dedicated analytics integration. A future production deployment may have hosting-related cookies or logs, and this policy will be updated to reflect actual practices.

Your choices

No user account is created through this preview. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into demonstration fields. Privacy requests and a verified contact channel will be provided before production launch.

Updates

We will revise this policy when the live integration is ready, and display an updated effective date.
