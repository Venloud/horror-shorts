# BLOODY MARY: WHERE DID THE NAME COME FROM?

Write a suspenseful Night Files folklore explainer about the uncertain origins of the Bloody Mary legend.

Center the story on the mystery of where the name and mirror ritual tradition came from. You may discuss commonly suggested historical or folkloric connections, but clearly label them as theories, associations, or disputed explanations rather than proven origin.

Do not turn this into a list. Follow one investigative thread and use concrete images: a mirror, an old story, a name repeated across generations, or a room where the legend is told.

The ending should make the viewer understand what is known, what is uncertain, and why the legend survived.

Do not invent sources, quotations, historical events, or certainty. Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people as characters, real brands, or named towns.
