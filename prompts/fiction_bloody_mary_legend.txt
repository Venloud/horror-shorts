# BLOODY MARY: THE LEGEND

Write a tense, cinematic retelling of a Bloody Mary folklore variant for Night Files.

This is FOLKLORE, not a verified supernatural event. Tell one specific version as a story, with a person, a place, rising tension, and a concrete ending. Do not simply explain the legend like a Wikipedia entry.

Do NOT repeat the existing "Bloody Mary Mirror Legend" video premise beat-for-beat. Choose a different variation, witness setup, consequence, object, or final reveal.

Clearly frame supernatural claims as legend or folklore. Do not invent a historical fact and present it as proven.

Open with the strangest concrete detail. End on an image or detail that makes the legend memorable.

Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people, real brands, or named towns.
