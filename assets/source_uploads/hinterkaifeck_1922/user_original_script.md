# Video Script: The Hinterkaifeck Mystery

**Format:** YouTube Short (~50–55 seconds)
**Tone:** Tense, atmospheric, unsettling whisper/narration

## [0:00 – 0:08] The Hook
**Visual:** Close-up of heavy boots trudging through thick, undisturbed snow toward a secluded wooden farmhouse surrounded by pine woods. Black-and-white / desaturated color grade.
**Audio / SFX:** Wind howling, crunching footsteps in deep snow, low sub-bass drone.
**Voiceover:**

"In March 1922, a Bavarian farmer found fresh footprints in the snow leading out of the woods and straight toward his home. But there were no prints leading back out."

## [0:08 – 0:20] The Buildup
**Visual:** Quick cut to dusty attic floorboards, flickering oil lamp light, and a stack of old newspapers on a rustic wooden table.
**Audio / SFX:** Creaking floorboards overhead, subtle ticking clock.
**Voiceover:**

"Over the next few days, the Gruber family heard heavy footsteps pacing in their attic. House keys vanished without a trace, and unfamiliar newspapers were found inside the locked home."

## [0:20 – 0:34] The Event
**Visual:** Shadowy interior of an old barn, hay scattered across stone pavers, silhouette of a farming mattock tool leaning against a wall.
**Audio / SFX:** Barn door creaking open, heartbeat sound effect slowly rising.
**Voiceover:**

"On March 31st, one by one, four family members were lured into the barn in total darkness. All six household members, including the maid who had arrived just hours earlier, were slain with a farm mattock."

## [0:34 – 0:48] The Terrifying Twist
**Visual:** Exterior of the isolated homestead at night. Smoke gently curling from the stone chimney. A lone window glowing with faint candlelight.
**Audio / SFX:** Distant, faint clinking of kitchenware, eerie ambient chime.
**Voiceover:**

"The most horrifying detail? Neighbors saw smoke rising from the chimney for days afterward. The killer stayed on the farm... eating their food and feeding the cattle, living side-by-side with the victims before vanishing forever."

## [0:48 – 0:55] The Outro & Call to Action
**Visual:** Archival photograph or aged illustration of the Hinterkaifeck farmhouse fading into darkness. Night Files logo watermark.
**Audio / SFX:** Wind whistle fading to silence.
**Voiceover:**

"Over a century later, the killer has never been identified. Who was hiding in the attic?"
