SCRIPT TRUE
Someone walked out of the frozen woods and straight toward their farmhouse. But the footprints never led back out.

That was the story a Bavarian farmer reportedly told in March nineteen twenty-two. Then came more strange warnings.

Footsteps supposedly echoed through the attic. A newspaper appeared that nobody recognized. And a house key was missing.

On March thirty-first, six people were murdered at Hinterkaifeck. Four bodies were found in the barn, and two inside the farmhouse, including the newly arrived maid.

A mattock, later recovered on the property, was identified as the likely weapon. But the strangest detail came afterward.

There were indications somebody had stayed after the killings. The animals had apparently been tended, and smoke was reported from the chimney.

Was the murderer hiding nearby before the attack? Or were these clues misunderstood?

Investigators never established who killed the six residents. More than one hundred years later, who was there remains a mystery.
