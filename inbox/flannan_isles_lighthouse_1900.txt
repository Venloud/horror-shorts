SCRIPT TRUE
Three lighthouse keepers vanished from a Scottish island. When the relief ship arrived, even a rocket fired into the sky brought no answer.

December 26, 1900.

Joseph Moore stepped ashore expecting to relieve his fellow keepers.

Instead, he found the lighthouse deserted. The beds were empty. The kitchen fire had gone cold.

But someone had finished the morning's work.

The lamp was prepared. The machinery was clean. Records showed the men had been working there on December 15.

So where had all three gone?

The most disturbing clue wasn't inside the lighthouse.

It was down by the sea.

At the western landing, iron railings were twisted. Ropes had been swept away. Even a massive stone had been dislodged.

Investigators believed the men had gone outside and been caught by an enormous wave.

But consider this.

Some of the damaged equipment stood more than a hundred feet above sea level.

Whatever struck that landing had reached that high.

No one witnessed what happened.

And not one of the three keepers was ever found.
