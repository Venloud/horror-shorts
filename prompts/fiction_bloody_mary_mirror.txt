# BLOODY MARY: WHY THE MIRROR?

Write a short folklore mystery about why mirrors became central to the Bloody Mary legend.

Do not give ritual instructions or encourage the viewer to perform the ritual. Instead, explore the eerie symbolism and folklore logic of seeing a face in a mirror.

Build the explanation around one concrete scene and one memorable visual. Distinguish interpretation from documented folklore. Do not claim that one explanation is the proven origin.

End with a chilling observation about what a mirror can make a person believe they are seeing.

Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people as characters, real brands, or named towns.
