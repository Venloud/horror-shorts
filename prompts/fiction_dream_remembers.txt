# THE DREAM THAT REMEMBERED ME

Write an original psychological horror story about an adult with a recurring dream that begins changing in response to things happening during waking life.

At first the changes are subtle. Then the dream includes details the narrator has never consciously noticed and eventually seems to remember the narrator's actions.

Keep the horror concrete and visual. Avoid a generic "it was all a dream" ending.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
