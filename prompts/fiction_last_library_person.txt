# THE LAST PERSON IN THE LIBRARY

Write an original atmospheric horror story about an adult who stays inside a library after closing.

The building appears empty, but books continue being checked out. Build tension through checkout records, footsteps, lights, doors, returned books, or other concrete details.

Do not use a generic ghost reveal. The final discovery should connect the library records to the narrator in an unsettling way.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
