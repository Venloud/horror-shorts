# BLOODY MARY: ORIGINAL FICTION SPIN-OFF

Write an ORIGINAL psychological horror story inspired by the Bloody Mary legend, but do not retell the standard mirror ritual.

Use the legend as the starting idea, then create new adult characters, a new setting, and a new rule or consequence. The story must stand on its own even if the viewer has never heard of Bloody Mary.

Do not claim the fictional events happened. Do not copy any existing Bloody Mary story.

The first sentence must create immediate curiosity. Build toward a concrete reveal that changes the meaning of an earlier detail. End with one short chilling line.

Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people, real brands, or named towns.
