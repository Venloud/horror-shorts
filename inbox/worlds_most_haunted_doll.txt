They said the doll was haunted. Nobody believed it until they developed the photographs.

In 1978, a family bought an antique porcelain doll at an estate sale.

Every morning, it appeared somewhere different.

Then their daughter found an old family photograph from 1923.

The doll was standing behind her great grandmother.

Same dress. Same cracked face.

That night, her father locked it in the basement.

At 3 AM, someone knocked on his bedroom door.

A little girl's voice whispered, "You forgot me."

He opened the door.

Nothing.

Then he heard his daughter screaming downstairs.

He ran to her bedroom.

She was asleep.

But beside her pillow was a photograph.

It showed him standing in the doorway.

And behind him, the doll was holding a knife.
