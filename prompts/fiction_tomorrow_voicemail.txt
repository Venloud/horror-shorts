# THE VOICEMAIL FROM TOMORROW

Write an original psychological horror story about an adult receiving a voicemail from their own phone number that describes an event that has not happened yet.

Make the prediction concrete and verifiable. The narrator should test or resist the message, causing the next messages to become more disturbing.

Avoid an easy time-travel explanation. The ending should leave a physical or recorded piece of evidence.

Fiction only. No real people, brands, or named towns. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, or harm to children.
