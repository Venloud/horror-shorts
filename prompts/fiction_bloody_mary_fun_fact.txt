# BLOODY MARY: ONE DARK FUN FACT

Create a short Night Files "fun fact" story about Bloody Mary folklore.

Use ONE genuinely interesting, widely documented fact, belief, historical connection, or folklore detail. Do not make a list of facts. Build the fact into a tiny scene or discovery so it feels like a horror short rather than a lecture.

Most important: distinguish FACT from LEGEND. If a connection is debated or commonly repeated but not proven, say so in the narration. Do not present the popular claim that Bloody Mary is definitively named after one historical person as settled history.

Hook with the surprising detail, explain why it matters, then end with a creepy image or question.

No invented citations, dates, names, or historical claims. Adults only. No gore, sexual content, self-harm, suicide, harm to children, real people as characters, real brands, or named towns.
