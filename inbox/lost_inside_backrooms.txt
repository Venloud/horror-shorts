The security camera showed him entering an empty office hallway.

He never came out.

At first, Marcus thought he'd taken the wrong exit.

But every door led to another yellow room.

The lights buzzed. The carpet was wet.

After six hours, he found his apartment door.

Same number. Same scratches.

Inside, his mother was cooking.

She smiled and said, "You're finally home."

Marcus almost cried.

Then he noticed the kitchen clock.

It had no hands.

His mother turned around.

Her face was perfectly still.

From somewhere behind the walls, he heard his real mother screaming.

"Marcus, don't eat anything!"

The woman at the stove slowly opened her mouth.

And every light in the building went out.
