SCRIPT
In 1891, a wealthy London family invited a medium into their home.

Their youngest daughter had been speaking to someone inside the walls.

During the séance, the medium asked the spirit its name.

The table shook.

A voice answered, "Edward."

The father went pale.

Edward was his name.

The medium asked when the spirit had died.

"Tomorrow."

Everyone laughed nervously.

Until the candles went out.

Something began knocking beneath the floorboards.

The medium screamed, "End the séance!"

But the father's chair was already empty.

The next morning, they found his body inside the wall.

The police estimated he'd been dead for at least thirty years.

And that evening, his daughter began speaking to someone new.
