SCRIPT
At 2:17 in the morning, someone knocked on my apartment door.
I live alone.
I checked the peephole. It was my neighbor, Daniel. He looked terrified.
I opened the door. He grabbed my arm and whispered, "Don't let me in."
Then he ran down the hallway.
I just stood there, confused. Until someone knocked again.
Same knock. Three slow taps.
I looked through the peephole. Daniel was standing there. Same clothes. Same terrified face.
He pointed at my bedroom. Then whispered through the door: "He's still in there."
I turned around. My bedroom door was open. I knew I'd closed it.
Then my phone buzzed. A text from Daniel.
"Whatever you do, don't turn around."
I froze. Because Daniel was still standing in front of me. And he was smiling now.
Behind him, at the end of the hallway, someone was walking toward us.
Wearing my clothes.
